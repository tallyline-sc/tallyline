# Accessibility report

> **Doc type:** Living reference — draft (JavaFX handoff stage 2; contrast and icon feature width so far). Last reviewed: 2026-11-03.

> **Note 2026-10-12:** [ADR-0005](../adr/0005-rust-and-egui.md) replaces JavaFX with egui. The contrast results below are computed on the token values and stand unchanged; the egui rendition replaces the mechanism the "rendered JavaFX sheets" row and the JavaFX rendering of icon strokes refer to (Modena, mechanism A, `SVGPath`), so those checks are re-aimed at the egui rendition once open point O-112 adopts it. The crate's own `tests/contrast.rs` checks text, focus ring and control borders against the same floors. The screen-reader part of the manual protocol is now `R-UI-17a`, a Could without a gate, spike or milestone (ADR-0005 D5, O-111); contrast, keyboard operation, text size and "colour is never the only signal" remain binding (R-UI-17). The generator named below (`Contrast.java`) is replaced by an `xtask` task once O-112 is settled; until then it stays the way to regenerate the generated section.

This report records the authoritative accessibility checks of the design system on the repository's token values, replacing the indicative figures Claude Design computed in its web previews. It covers the floors of R-UI-17: text at least 4.5:1 (7:1 in high contrast), state indicators, focus rings and other marks at least 3:1, in every theme.

**Scope of the contrast run.** Every pairing the system declares (575), plus derived checks:
- the focus ring on every surface, deviation background, selection tint and hover tint;
- every confidence border on every deviation background, the selection tint and the hover tint;
- the major-deviation Δ badge;
- the focus ring on the primary and the danger fill;
- the three text colours on every surface and state ground;
- the dark theme's luminance band.

The script that wrote the section below (`Contrast.java`) was removed on 2026-10-12 (ADR-0005 D12); its replacement is an `xtask` task, and the Tallyline egui crate carries its own contrast tests (`tests/contrast.rs`, passed on 2026-10-12 in a build outside the repository). The section below is the last generated result and stays valid for the token values it names.

### Icon feature width

Run 2026-10-09 (SP-5) on the 156 glyphs of the "Icons used" table of version `1791542897-a5d3`, from `@material-symbols/svg-400@0.47.6` (npm tarball, sha512 integrity as published by the registry; SHA-256 `0a74f3a0f4562a34190a89902fa99dba41f3796c0b79e500065b8dcb2553c01f`), style `rounded`, `-fill`. The tarball sits in a scratch directory and is not vendored; vendoring is stage 3.

**Method.** Each path is filled with the non-zero rule and rasterised without anti-aliasing at 240 × 240 px (4 units of the 960-unit grid per pixel). The local thickness of a pixel is the diameter of the largest inscribed disc that contains it (exact Euclidean distance transform); it is measured on the ink and on the gaps, with the area outside the icon counted as gap, and scaled to a 16 px icon. Per glyph the 5th percentile is reported, so that single pixels at pointed tips do not decide.

**Result: the brief's minimum of about 1.5 px at 16 px is not met.** The strokes of wght 400 at opsz 48 are about 60 units wide, 1.0 px at 16 px and 1.25 px at 20 px.

| At 16 px, 5th percentile | below 1.5 px | below 1.2 px | below 1.0 px |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink (strokes) | 105 of 156 | 100 | 74 |
| Gaps (counters, cut-outs) | 51 of 156 | 40 | 17 |

Thinnest ink: `hdr_off` and `hdr_on` 0.73 px, `rule_settings` 0.79 px, `fiber_new` and `history_toggle_off` 0.87 px. Narrowest gaps: `point_of_sale` 0.47 px, `fiber_new` 0.73 px, `block` and `help` 0.88 px, `help_center` 0.95 px. Filled glyphs such as `info`, `error` and `schedule` keep their ink but have gaps of about 1 px. **Comparison with wght 600** (`@material-symbols/svg-600@0.47.6`, fetched the same way with the owner's approval; SHA-256 `76a5dec392c84dabe396030ecce703c9ae110aa6da678ec46d9f45fe90fc5bbb`; same method). Its strokes are about 1.3 px at 16 px. The heavier weight thickens the strokes but narrows the gaps, so no combination meets the floor for every glyph:

| Glyphs below 1.5 px (5th percentile) | Ink at 16 px | Gaps at 16 px | Ink at 20 px | Gaps at 20 px |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| wght 400 | 105 | 51 | 100 | 40 |
| wght 600 | 104 | 57 | 59 | 39 |

**Decision (O-107, @greluc, 2026-10-09):** the icons stay at wght 400. Instead of the fixed 1.5 px floor, the manual protocol checks the anti-aliased JavaFX rendering at 100, 125 and 150 %, and a glyph that is not legible there is replaced through Claude Design; the first candidates are `point_of_sale`, `hdr_on`, `hdr_off`, `fiber_new` and `rule_settings`. Not yet checked: how JavaFX's anti-aliased `SVGPath` renders these widths at 100 %, 125 % and 150 % (stage 4, manual protocol).

**Note 2026-10-12:** under ADR-0005 the icons are glyphs of a font rendered by egui, not JavaFX `SVGPath`, so the rendering check at 100, 125 and 150 % is repeated on the egui rendition; the measured geometric widths above stand. The delivered crate's icon font is modified and subset (open condition of O-112).
